What it is

One front door, one sign-in, one map

HAHBAH (Boston for “harbor”) is the home network’s control room. Every app gets a friendly name like replexon.hahbah.com or plex.hahbah.com, all behind a single sign-in, with Google or a backup password. The dashboard shows the whole homelab as a transit map. Machines are hub stations, apps are stops, and every kind of connection is a colored line. When something breaks, its line goes into “service alert” the way a real subway would. The names only work inside the house, so nothing is exposed to the internet.

How it works

The route a request takes

hahbah.comNames point at the mini PC’s address on the home network (Porkbun DNS).
CaddyThe front door: automatic HTTPS for every name, and a sign-in check before any app.
PortalSign-in, this dashboard, and the health and certificate checks every 30 seconds.
The appsPlex, RePlexOn, Sample Service, Tautulli: one sign-in covers them all.
CollectorA separate container for checks that need secrets: NAS stats over a locked-down SSH key, the domain and DNS records, backup history. The web-facing portal never holds a key.
TautulliWatches Plex and keeps the play history behind the Plex card and the drive-in movie screen in the 3D view.
